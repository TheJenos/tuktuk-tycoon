Privacy Policy

Tuk-Tuk Tycoon · Last updated October 24, 2026

Tuk-Tuk Tycoon is a small free property-trading board game, playable on its website (tuktuk-tycoon.netlify.app) and as a Discord Activity. This policy explains what data the game uses. In short: only what's needed to play a game, kept only while the game needs it, and never sold or used for ads. Playing offline against bots sends nothing to us at all.

What we collect and why

DataWhyHow long
Your player name: typed on the website, or your Discord display name in the Discord Activity Shown to the other players at your table (seats, players panel, game log) Until the room is deleted
Game data: your seat, token and colour, cash, properties, buildings, the moves you make (rolls, purchases, bids, trades) and the game's move log Needed to run the game and keep every player's board in sync Until the room is deleted: a waiting room when its last player leaves, a game in progress at most 24 hours after its last move
Room details: room name, code, rules, whether it is public, and the names of the players in it To list open rooms and let others join or watch Until the room is deleted
Presence: whether you are connected, and whether the game tab is visible To show who is online and to hand the room's chores (bots, timers) to a connected player Removed when you disconnect
Discord account basics (Discord Activity only): your Discord user ID, username and display name via the identify permission, and rpc.activities.write to show the game on your Discord profile To sign you in, seat you at your voice channel's table, and show "Playing Tuk-Tuk Tycoon" Your Discord user ID is part of your player ID while the room exists; the access token is not stored by us
Data on your device: your player name, settings, an offline saved game, and which online rooms you can rejoin So the game remembers your preferences and lets you continue a game In your browser's local storage until you clear it

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no cookies.
  • No real money: the game's rupees are play money only.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone beyond the services below that run the game.
  • We don't access your Discord messages, servers, friends list or email address.

Services that process data for us

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database (Singapore region) stores the online game data described above. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Netlify hosts the website and the small server function used for Discord sign-in. Like any web host, it processes technical request data such as IP addresses. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Discord, when you play the Activity. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Discord.

Who can see your data

Everything at a table (names, cash, properties and moves) is visible to the other players and to anyone watching the room, and the list of open rooms is public. Please don't use personal information such as your real full name as your player name.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Discord requires its users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires).

Your choices

You can leave a room at any time. You can clear the game's saved settings and games by clearing your browser's site data. To ask about your data or request deletion, message thejenos on Discord.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: message thejenos on Discord.
