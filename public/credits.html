Credits

Tuk-Tuk Tycoon is a light-hearted property-trading board game set in Sri Lanka, made for fun.

The game

The rules follow a classic 2007 property-trading rulebook (with the Speed Die), in the game's own words. Every name, card and joke on the board is original. The board, tokens, dice and logo are drawn by the game itself.

Graphics

None to credit: the board, the tokens, the dice, the cards, the icons and every texture are generated by the game's own code, and text is drawn in your system's fonts. Nothing is downloaded from other sites.
